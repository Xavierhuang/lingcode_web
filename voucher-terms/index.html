Promotional terms

LingCode Pro voucher terms

30-day trial, then $20 per month automatically until canceled. A payment card is required. Cancel before the trial ends to avoid the first charge.

Eligibility and limits

Vouchers are available only to new or eligible LingCode Free subscribers. Active, trialing, past-due, and unpaid subscribers are not eligible. There is a limit of one voucher per account and payment card. We may block or revoke redemption associated with misuse, resale, automated guessing, or fraud.

Subscription and cancellation

Redeeming a valid voucher starts a 30-day LingCode Pro trial after Stripe confirms Checkout and the payment method. When the trial ends, the subscription automatically renews at $20 per month plus applicable taxes until canceled. You may cancel through the LingCode billing portal at any time; cancel before the trial ends to avoid a charge. Cancellation takes effect according to the billing portal confirmation.

Voucher expiration

The voucher must be redeemed before the date printed on the card. An expired, redeemed, blocked, or revoked voucher cannot be replaced or reactivated. Checkout must be completed while its reservation remains valid.

No cash value

Vouchers have no cash value, are not refundable, and cannot be exchanged for cash, account credit, or another promotion. Lost, copied, damaged, or publicly disclosed codes may not be replaced.

Privacy and payment handling

Stripe collects and processes payment details. LingCode does not store full card numbers. LingCode stores only security-safe voucher records and a keyed, non-reversible representation of a card fingerprint to enforce the one-card limit.
